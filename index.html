Chapter 1 na mahatva na 5 padh (yaad rakho)

  1. Investment = tapas + mool rakam suraksha + yogya kamai.
  2. Speculation mate fakt max 10%, alag account.
  3. Bazaar nu bhavishya jani na shakay. Vahenchi ne (diversified) rakho.
  4. Jhadpi kamai na formulas thi dur raho.
  5. Price ≠ Value. Company ni asli kimat juo.
