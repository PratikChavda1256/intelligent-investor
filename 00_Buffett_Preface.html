📖 Warren Buffett nu Preface

Book: The Intelligent Investor  |  Pages: viii (shuruaat)

Paragraph 1

I read the first edition of this book early in 1950, when I was nineteen. I thought then that it was by far the best book about investing ever written. I still think it is.
Me aa book ni pehli edition 1950 ni shuruaat ma vanchi hati, tyare hu 19 varsh no hato. Tyare mane lagyu hatu ke investing vishe lakhayeli aa sauthi saari book chhe. Aaje pan mane evu j lage chhe.

Paragraph 2

To invest successfully over a lifetime does not require a stratospheric IQ, unusual business insights, or inside information. What's needed is a sound intellectual framework for making decisions and the ability to keep emotions from corroding that framework.
Jindagi bhar saru investment karva mate bov ooncho IQ, khaas business ni samaj, ke andar ni gupt mahiti ni jarur nathi. Jarur chhe: nirnay levani majboot vicharsarani ane lagnio (emotions) e e vicharsarani ne bagadi na nakhe tevi shakti.

Paragraph 3

This book precisely and clearly prescribes the proper framework. You must supply the emotional discipline.
Aa book barabar ane spasht rite yogya framework batave chhe. Emotional discipline (lagnio par kaabu) tamare lavvo padse.

Paragraph 4

If you follow the behavioral and business principles that Graham advocates--and if you pay special attention to the invaluable advice in Chapters 8 and 20--you will not get a poor result from your investments.
Jo tame Graham e batavela vartan ane business na siddhanto nu paalan karso, ane Chapter 8 ane 20 ni anmol salah par khaas dhyan aapso, to tamara investment ma kharab parinam nahi aave.
💡 Yaad rakho: Chapter 8 (Market Fluctuations) ane Chapter 20 (Margin of Safety) sauthi mahatva na chhe.

Paragraph 5

The sillier the market's behavior, the greater the opportunity for the business-like investor. Follow Graham and you will profit from folly rather than participate in it.
Bazaar jetlo vadhare murkhamibharelo vartan kare, tetli j vadhare taak samajdar investor ne made chhe. Graham ne follow karso to tame bazaar ni murkhai no fayado uthavso, tema bhagidar nahi bano.
